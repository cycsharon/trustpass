NovaPay Ltd - AI Governance Policy (version 2.1)
Approved by the Board of Directors on 3 March 2027. Next review: March 2028.

1. Purpose
This written AI policy, approved by management and the board, sets out how NovaPay builds, buys and runs AI systems.

2. Accountability
The Chief Technology Officer is the named person accountable for each AI system, and every model has a responsible owner recorded in the AI register.
We keep an up-to-date AI inventory that lists every AI model, its purpose and its AI supplier, reviewed every quarter.

3. Data and privacy
Our data map records which personal data each model uses and the purpose of collection, and our personal information collection statement (PICS) now covers AI.
Under our contracts, AI suppliers are not permitted to use our customers' data for training their models.

4. Model risk
Every model is tested for accuracy against a hold-out set before launch, and the signed test report is kept on file.
Production models are monitored on a drift dashboard, which raises an alert when performance falls below threshold.

5. Security and suppliers
Each AI supplier passes a due diligence assessment covering security and data use before we sign a contract.
Access to models and training data is restricted to named roles, protected by MFA, and every access is logged.
We have not yet run a red-team exercise on our chatbot; one is planned for Q3 2027.

6. Human oversight
A trained analyst reviews every high-impact AI decision, such as blocking a merchant payment, before it reaches the customer.

7. Transparency and incidents
Customers are informed that they are chatting with an AI assistant through a notice at the start of every chat.
All AI incidents are recorded in an AI incident log with the impact, the fix and the date closed.
